Snap photographs the page you are on, lets you draw on it, and puts the finished picture on your clipboard. That is all it does.

Press the toolbar button or Shift+Alt+S. The page is captured and the markup surface drops over it. Draw boxes, arrows and text notes in red, amber, green or purple. A box can be a ring, a wash, or solid. Every mark stays an object until you copy: click to pick it up, drag to move, pull a corner to resize, recolour, delete, undo. Press Copy, Enter or C and the picture is on your clipboard with the page's address drawn across the top, ready to paste into Slack, a document, a design tool or an issue.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. There is no account, no server and no tracking. The screenshot exists in the tab and on your clipboard, and nowhere else.

Settings let you choose the theme, where the surface opens, what happens after copying, whether the address bar is drawn, and the starting ink.
