# Snap privacy policy

Snap is a Chrome extension that takes a screenshot of the current tab, lets
you draw on it, and copies the result to your clipboard.

**Snap does not collect, store, or transmit any data.** The one thing it
handles is the screenshot of the page you are on (website content), and it
handles it only on your own machine.

- The screenshot exists only in the memory of the tab it was taken in and,
  once you press Copy, on your clipboard. The extension never writes it to
  disk and never sends it anywhere.
- Your settings (theme, default ink, where the surface opens) are stored in
  Chrome's extension storage, which Chrome may sync between your own devices
  under your Google account. Snap has no server and never sees them.
- Snap makes no network requests of any kind.
- Snap requests no access to any website. It acts only on the tab you press
  its button on, at that moment, under Chrome's `activeTab` permission.

If this policy changes, the extension's version number will change and this
page will be updated.
